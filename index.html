My purchase didn't arrive

Open Settings in the game and tap Restore purchases. "No Ads" and other one-time purchases are restored from your App Store or Google Play account.

How do I change ad consent?

In the game, go to Settings > Privacy options (shown where consent is required).

Will I lose my progress?

Your progress is stored on your device. Deleting the game also deletes your progress.
